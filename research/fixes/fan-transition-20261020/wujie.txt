FINAL STATIC FOLLOW-UP — request invariants and no-op power boundary
Kind::EcManual has exactly two construction paths in this translation unit:
ecManual() immediately supplies savedProfiles, and restoreSavedProfiles() also
sets savedProfiles before request(). Restore/adopt/CLI callers use those APIs;
there is no direct external access to private Impl::Request. Thus the repeated
Manual comparison's savedProfiles dereference is valid for all current paths.
The worker'srevision is single-thread-owned. sequence/pending and publicstate
are accessed under the controller mutex. A current explicit command retains its
DeviceOperation while publishing; request admission refuses applying/pending and
tryBegin cannot acquire a second operation. Old periodic state/unknown/error
publications are the concurrent case handled by this patch. A no-op completion
of an explicit command cannot normally be superseded through current admission.
Root-approved final addition puts checkPower() inside the no-op publication mutex,
matching the existing full-apply commit lock order (controller mutex→checkPower).
If the epoch changes after flags readback, PowerInterrupted unwinds the lock
before entering the existing interrupted/recovery handler; it cannot falsely
complete the no-op across suspend. No new EC call/write or global lock introduced.
