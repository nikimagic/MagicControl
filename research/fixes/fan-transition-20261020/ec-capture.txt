EC capture scheduling and timing — 2026-10-20

Scope: common dump orchestrator plus verified Wujie EcAccess transport and
optional local EC getters. No driver/IOCTL, register writer or fan-map changes.

The existing Wujie dump required97.797s and98.652s for two complete4096-byte
snapshots. It had no read retries/errors. Repeated slow address pages alone do
not establish contention or a faulty driver. No speedup is claimed without a
new hardware capture.

Added ec-read-timing.json and ec-after-read-timing.json. Every attempted16-byte
read records address/count, outcome and elapsed microseconds, including failed
or busy attempts. Wujie additionally separates local gate wait, backend/cross-
process gate wait, actual raw-byte I/O, successful bytes and slowest-byte address.
Other typed transports retain end-to-end timing and explicitly null detailed
transport timing; no fabricated mutex/I/O breakdown is inferred.

A nestable process-local PassiveEcReadPause is independent of ObservationPause
and DeviceOperation admission. Only after confirmed firmware Default, and only
for the snapshot scope, optional parent-process EC power/battery/fan telemetry
getters defer reads. The dump remains allowed to read; the two transaction gates
are unchanged. RAII releases the hint on normal completion/cancellation/error.
Active online cooling, safety/ownership checks, cleanup and commands are never
paused by this hint. Other providers (MSR/SMBus/storage/GPU/Windows battery IOCTL)
and isolated diagnostic-helper telemetry remain independent. Already running
reads may finish; this is neither an atomic EC snapshot nor a global driver pause.
ReadOnly/unconfirmed Default snapshots do not set the hint.

All4096 addresses, valid masks, retry/cancellation behavior,180-second budget,
16-byte lock holds and2ms inter-block fairness remain. Default→snapshot→telemetry
and30-secondMax sequencing, minimum60-second telemetry and finalDefault policy
are preserved. No prior Manual/online mode is automatically replayed after dump.

Protected cards/fonts/layout/scrollbar are unchanged. Compilation and static
artifact/source inspections are reported separately by root; no application,
GUI, test suite or hardware experiment is run for these changes.
