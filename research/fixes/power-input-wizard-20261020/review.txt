PowerInputDump.cpp:48-51 now admits stage 2 from known online state (line 47), SOC >=99.5 and finite |watts| <=0.05, without a charging/discharging flag veto. The previous flow==discharging veto could reject a valid full/zero-rate baseline for the very flag anomaly this procedure is intended to investigate. It is absent in the final source inspected.

CORRECTED P2-2: abandoned Windows worker could share mutable session with a new worker
Windows BiosPage.cpp:197-201 now cancels the old task AND resets powerSession before dropping the job. The old detached worker retains its captured shared_ptr (line 427); a later invocation creates a different session (line 413). Thus two workers cannot mutate the same nextStage/attempt/attempts/checkpoint state after a preview/profile change. The previous wait on the abandoned task's hardwareGate alone was insufficient because PowerInputDump.cpp:110 supplies a new nested dump gate.
Normal defer/resume remains same-process and occurs after task completion publication: Windows worker done.store(release) line 457; completion reads acquire. Linux keeps the active dump job through profile refresh and does not admit a second job while it exists.
