Mechanism
Public activeLanguage/languageIndex/setLanguage/Entry/translations/translate API
is preserved. Added shared formatMessage(source, initializer_list<pair<view,view>>).
Named placeholders{name} are substituted exactly once after language selection;
explicit caller arguments are opaque. Semantic arguments must be translated by
the caller before passing them; model names,owners,paths,IDs remain unchanged.

translate() first uses whole-key matching (including exact English aliases for
our own English backend errors). Protected names Unknown/Default/Manual/Max/
Manual online/FANs/SETs/KEYs/BIOS/CPU=GPU/CPU≠GPU retain their product spellings.
Windows/UNC/sysfs/home/tmp/PCI/USB/HID/ACPI/HTTP paths are not translated.

Reviewed anchored templates support existing concatenated messages. Whole
structured rows split only on explicit bullet/semicolon/newline boundaries;
a segment must be a known message/field/number or an opaque non-Cyrillic value.
One unknown Cyrillic segment preserves the whole row. Unknown sentences do not
undergo the old per-word replacement (e.g. no longer produce “No показаний”).
Whole exact field-label + colon and numeric label readouts keep existing common
cards working without duplicating any page or altering geometry.

Inferred detail/effect/mode/state slots are separately reviewed display values.
Quantity slots total/free/limit/enforced/minimum/maximum/default use ONLY the
strict quantity parser: full scalar + known unit, or complete reviewed pair/range.
No general message recursion for quantity slots; no translation of arbitrary
model/owner/path/ID arguments. Capacity pairs such as99.07Wh /82.04Wh use the
same quantity path. Numeric bytes/grouping/decimal formatting are not changed by
this code: locale display formatting belongs to the shared DisplayFormat owner.
Atomic units Wh/Hz/V/GB/GiB/MiB/MHz etc keep physical and binary scales distinct.
