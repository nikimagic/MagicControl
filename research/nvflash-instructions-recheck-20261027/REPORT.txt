NVFlash read order review,27October2026
Research only: no source/binary/settings changes, no vendor/hardware execution.

Conclusion
Standalone ordinary --save does not require a preceding separate --check.
Vendor/OEM instructions and published scripts perform backup directly after
adapter discovery. Two ordinary reads and SHA256/shape checks are our extra
collector verification, not a prerequisite prescribed by the OEM manuals.
--check means checking supported EEPROM, not validating a ROM file checksum.
A recognized part geometry remains required for OUR full-EEPROM completeness
claim. Ordinary result and entire result must remain independent.

Primary evidence reviewed online
1. Fastwel VIM556/VIM556RC manufacturer manual,version1.2,October2015,
section5.1,p33(zerobasedPDF32): directly save the original NVIDIA firmware,
then preserve backup; write-protection changes occur only afterwards in its
separate flashing procedure. No preliminary check is stated for save.
https://www.fastwel.com/upload/iblock/124/VIM556_v1.2_2manual.pdf
Scope: older Quadro/MXM product, not GM6/RTX4060 certification.

2. cyclone-github/nvidia_vbios author's Linux procedure states Debian11/12/13
usage and list followed by save. Its later flashing steps are outside our
read-only scope and were neither run nor copied into MagicControl.
https://github.com/cyclone-github/nvidia_vbios/blob/main/nvflash_how_to_linux.txt

3. danindiana/rtx3080-rebar-vbios rebar-enable.sh,master, backup block before
its check block. README identifies LinuxNVFlash5.867.0. Published session log
has a successful999424-byte post-flash read. This is an author's report and
source evidence for that desktopGPU, not independent GM6 reproduction.
The project's interpretation of check as ROM compatibility is NOT adopted;
its write/driver-unload/kill sequence is NOT part of our implementation.
https://github.com/danindiana/rtx3080-rebar-vbios/blob/master/rebar-enable.sh
https://github.com/danindiana/rtx3080-rebar-vbios/blob/master/flash-session.log

Exact local payload evidence
research/nvflash-save-scope-20261025/windows-embedded-help.txt:
check is supported-EEPROM detection; save reads to a file.
entire-full-embedded-help.txt distinguishes raw compare from flashing-style
verify transformations. Prior exact Windows/Linux5.867 disassembly establishes
--entire offset0/capacity and16MiB cap, separately from ordinary ROM length.
Current source preserves EEPROM admission before entire, exact length, two
identical reads and raw compare. Unsupported status-register text alone does
not refuse a valid ID/geometry. No protectoff/override/reset/flash is added.
No current complete official NVIDIA5.867 public manual was located; historical
forum help and OEM manuals do not prove new flags or all GPU compatibility.

Important correction of confidence
GM6 archive19 ends at eeprom_capacity after check exit0. It has no crash event
or termination reason. The previous collector source ALREADY proceeded to
ordinary save even when capacity.admitted was false. Moving ordinary save
before check is a justified separation and removes exposure to the optional
probe before basic backup, but is NOT proof of the cause or cure of the old
abrupt termination. Only owner-run new evidence or an offline reproduced
failure could establish that. No successful MagicControl GM6 ROM capture is
claimed by this review. Current0.1.233 still needs that validation.
