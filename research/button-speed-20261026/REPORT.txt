Core source review:
BatteryChargeWindows uses stored WMI access,upper/lower/context and repeated
confirmation,including a final persistent/live check. Its only explicit pauses
here are50ms after a stored write,25ms between matches or250ms for mismatches.
Those alone do not explain the~2.06s difference; logged address-specific calls do.
No checks,rollback,lower autosave,persistence,platform or ROM-ID were changed.
Do not change to7/04 just for speed or replace fresh security/context checks
with a stale global ROM-ID cache.
