MagicControl bundles the variable Noto Sans SC and Noto Sans TC fonts for app-private UI use.
Sources: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanssc
         https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanstc
The matching SIL Open Font Licenses are included beside the fonts.
The app loads these fonts privately; they are not installed system-wide.
Traditional Chinese uses Noto Sans TC; Russian, English and Simplified Chinese keep Noto Sans SC.
